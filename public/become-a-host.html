Become a Host

Run a private, invite-only tournament at your venue for adults you know, with URBANO Gaming keeping the bracket, the rosters and the results.

About the pilot program

Hosting is by application, not automatic. URBANO reviews every application. Approval is at URBANO's discretion and may be revoked.

The first program supports one private, invite-only pilot competition per approved host.

  • Known adults only: everyone who plays, scores, or helps is an adult you know.
  • Four teams with at least four players each.
  • Two semifinals and one final.
  • Independent scorekeepers: each match is scored by someone who is not playing in it.
  • You share the invitation links yourself, by hand. Nothing is sent automatically.

The pilot does not include entry fees, prizes, sponsorships, minors, public statistics, automated invitations, or open registration.

Approval lets you create and run this one pilot. It does not make you an URBANO employee, partner, or certified organizer, and it is not a permanent affiliation.

Anyone can read this page. To apply you need to sign in with URBANO and have a display name.
