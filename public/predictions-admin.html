Venue Activations

MatchVenueEnabledPrize tiers

Progressive match: members attempt Easy first, then Medium, Hard and Legendary, and can send after completing any level. A venue offers a consecutive set of levels starting at Level 1 (Easy; Easy + Medium; Easy + Medium + Hard; or all four): with no prizes, or with a gap, it takes no participations. A member wins only the prize of the highest level reached. From the first prediction at a venue its levels, labels and highest level can no longer change.

Result

The score is the score after 90 minutes plus stoppage time. Goal events — select the scoring Player (own goals still select the Player who touched it last; the credited Team is derived automatically). Goals after minute 90 (extra time, penalties) are kept as evidence but do not count toward the score or toward any prediction.
